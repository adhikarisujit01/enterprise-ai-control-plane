# Public research basis

This project is an independent portfolio prototype. It uses public research to frame the problem, while all synthetic data, score weights, thresholds, governance tiers, recommendation logic, and implementation choices are original to this repository.

## 1. BCG — Client Zero: Inside BCG's AI Transformation

**Published:** November 11, 2026  
**URL:** https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/bcg-ai-transformation-client-zero

Relevant design themes:

- broad AI adoption does not by itself create sustained value;
- organizations should redesign workflows and roles around AI rather than merely automate tasks;
- decentralized innovation benefits from common architecture and standards;
- reusable context and capabilities can reduce duplicated effort;
- as agents take on more work, human ownership and accountability remain essential;
- transformation is primarily about people, processes, and ways of working in addition to algorithms and technology.

Prototype response:

- Executive Portfolio and Transformation Roadmap
- Target Operating Model / RACI
- Control Plane
- Agent Registry
- Scenario Simulator

## 2. BCG — Enterprise AI Control Plane: The CIO's Guide to Governing and Accelerating AI Agents

**Published:** August 14, 2026  
**URL:** https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/how-cios-govern-ai-agents-at-scale

Relevant design themes:

- fragmented platform-by-platform governance creates security, cost, and operational challenges;
- enterprise agent governance benefits from centralized identity, policy, visibility, and controls;
- standardized production paths can support speed and governance simultaneously;
- enterprise-wide ownership is important when agents span multiple platforms and business units.

Prototype response:

- identity mode and least-privilege checks
- permission-risk scoring
- policy / orchestration / observability control-plane model
- kill-switch simulation
- ownership and escalation views

## 3. BCG — Operationalizing AI Governance Gives Banks an Advantage in Scaling AI

**Published:** November 13, 2026  
**URL:** https://www.bcg.com/press/13november2026-operationalizing-ai-governance-advantage-scaling-ai

Relevant design theme:

Governance policy and day-to-day operational controls can mature at different speeds. The project therefore distinguishes between broad governance posture and concrete controls such as human override, audit logging, kill switches, identity scope, and least privilege.

## 4. BCG Platinion — AI Tech Consultant profile

**URL:** https://www.bcgplatinion.com/careers/roles/ai-tech-consultants

Relevant capability themes:

- analysis and evaluation of technology capabilities;
- feasibility and architecture thinking;
- digitization of business processes;
- business cases;
- requirements and MVP / POC support;
- organizational and procedural aspects of IT management;
- client-facing implementation support.

This prototype deliberately spans strategy, architecture, economics, governance, operating model, and implementation sequencing to demonstrate that intersection.
